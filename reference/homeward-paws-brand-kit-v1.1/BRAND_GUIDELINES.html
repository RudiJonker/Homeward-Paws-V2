Ten rules that keep it on brand

  1. Use the tokens. Never type a hex value, font size or radius by hand; use --hp-* variables, the Tailwind preset or the app theme.
  2. Teal (primary) is the action colour: main buttons, links, active tab, focus ring.
  3. Coral (accent) is warmth only: the logo heart, notification dot, illustration. Never text, never a button, never status.
  4. Status is always colour + icon + UPPERCASE label, and status colours mean status only.
  5. The status is MISSING, not "lost". The app covers all animals, not only "pets".
  6. Warm cream page (background), white cards with a hairline border, soft navy shadows; never harsh black shadows.
  7. Nunito for everything (400–900). Dancing Script only for short photo captions on the website.
  8. Use the supplied logo files. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects.
  9. Animal photos are the hero on report screens; marketing photography stays on the website and onboarding.
  10. Accessible by default: 4.5:1 text contrast, 48 dp touch targets, labels on every icon button, no flashing.
Brand

Warm, calm and practical

Homeward Paws is a free, Android-first community app for South Africa that helps reunite missing, found and sighted animals with their people. A product of Digitack (Pty) Ltd.

Personality

Warm, compassionate, trustworthy, calm, community-driven and practical. Friendly without being childish.

Not clinical, corporate, cluttered, or a constant emergency dashboard.

Tagline

Helping lost animals find their way home.

"Lost" is fine in this marketing line; in the product the status word is always MISSING. Celebration ("Every animal deserves to come home") is reserved for confirmed reunions.

Logo

Roof, heart, dog and cat

A teal roofline with chimney and a coral heart over navy dog and cat silhouettes. All text is converted to outlines, so the files never depend on installed fonts. SVG masters and PNGs are in assets/brand/.

logo-horizontal · default
logo-stacked · splash, onboarding, print
logo-mark · app icon, square spaces
logo-small · below 32 px, notifications
…-reverse · on teal or dark photos
logo-mark-mono-white · single colour
logo-mark-mono-navy · single colour
logo-mark-reverse · on navy

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least the heart's width on every side.
  • Use logo-small anywhere under 32 px. Keep the horizontal logo at least 120 px wide on screen.
  • Use only full colour, reverse or single colour.
  • Put the reverse version on teal, navy or photos (with a darkening scrim).

Don't

  • Redraw the logo in code, retype the wordmark or swap the font.
  • Recolour, stretch, rotate, crop or rearrange parts.
  • Add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects.
  • Use coral anywhere in the logo except the heart.
  • Place the full-colour logo on busy photos.

Building blocks

Live examples from css/homeward-paws.css. The app has matching React Native components (names from the design system in brackets).

Buttons (PrimaryButton, SecondaryButton, TextButton)

One primary per view. Urgent status buttons are scarce, clearly labelled and never flashing. Disable and show progress while submitting. Minimum height 52 px (web), 48 dp (app).

Imagery

The animal comes first

On report and case screens the reported animal's photo is the hero. Emotive photography (a dog at sunset) belongs on the website, onboarding, empty states and store listing only. Text over a photo always gets a darkening scrim.

Animal placeholders (no photo yet)

Illustrations

Map markers

Pins anchor bottom-centre; the approximate-area marker anchors centre and is used whenever public precision is suburb or approximate. Never show a private location as an exact pin.

Do

  • Rounded 4:3 or square crops (lg radius), real animals, natural light.
  • Flat illustration in brand colours for empty and success states.
  • Lazy-load and compress photos.

Don't

  • Stock photos inside the app.
  • Essential text over busy images.
  • Celebration art anywhere except a confirmed reunion.
Voice & words

Calm, kind, specific

Plain South African English. Short sentences. Tell people exactly what to do next. Never alarmist, never cutesy on a missing case.

Using the kit

For developers

Website

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/homeward-paws.css">

Tokens: tokens/tokens.css (--hp-*, plus aliases the live site uses). Tailwind: tokens/tailwind.preset.js. SCSS: tokens/tokens.scss.

The live site runs a strict Content Security Policy: no inline scripts (put JavaScript in .js files), self-hosted fonts and scripts, no analytics, cookies or third-party requests.

App (Expo, JavaScript only)

import { colors, typography, radii, shadows, status } from './theme';
<Text style={[typography.status, { color: status.missing.onTint }]}>
  {status.missing.label}
</Text>

Drop-in theme: react-native/theme/ (matches src/theme/, adds partner/admin, label and status type, and a status map). Icons, splash and adaptive icons: assets/app/.

Email

Cream background, one white card (20 px radius), teal button, navy text, logo assets/email/email-logo.png hosted on the website. Footer: Digitack (Pty) Ltd, info@digitack.co.za, privacy link. No tracking pixels.

Changing the brand

Edit tokens/tokens.json only, then run python3 tools/generate-tokens.py . to regenerate every format. Brand changes are approved by Andre (and Rudi for anything in the app); bump the version and date.
